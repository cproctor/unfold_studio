My City Story

Submitted: 2026-02-09 · Read full story

Story excerpt

Sam had always wanted to explore the city alone. The subway map crinkled
in her hands as she stepped off the train at 42nd Street.

The buildings rose up like canyon walls. She took a deep breath of
pretzel-cart air and grinned.

She wandered south, past food trucks and street musicians. A woman handed
her a flyer for a poetry slam...
